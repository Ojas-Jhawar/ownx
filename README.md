# Ownx

### The Ownership Passport for Physical Assets.

Ownx gives every physical product a digital identity — keeping its **purchase details, warranty, documents, service history, and ownership** in one place.

From the moment you buy something to the moment you sell or transfer it, Ownx keeps the complete ownership story together.

**[Live Demo →](https://own-x.netlify.app)**

---

## ✨ Features

* 🪪 **Ownership Passport** — Create a digital record for your physical assets.
* 🤖 **AI Invoice Extraction** — Upload an invoice and let AI extract product details automatically.
* 🧾 **Document Vault** — Keep invoices, receipts, and service documents organized.
* 🛠️ **Service History** — Track maintenance, repairs, costs, and service records.
* 🔗 **Share Your Passport** — Share a public, read-only ownership record.
* 🔄 **Transfer Ownership** — Easily transfer an asset to a new owner.
* 🏷️ **Resale** — Turn your ownership passport into a trusted resale listing.
* 🛍️ **Marketplace** — Discover assets being offered for resale.

---

## 🚀 The Idea

**Ownx makes ownership portable.**

Instead of losing an item's history across emails, receipts, folders, and messages, everything lives in one persistent **Ownership Passport**.

```text
Buy → Record → Maintain → Share → Resell → Transfer
```
